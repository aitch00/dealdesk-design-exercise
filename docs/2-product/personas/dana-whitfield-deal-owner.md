## Work context
- A director who leads Project Harbor and two other deals [ASSUMPTION].
- Often in meetings or travelling; answers in short breaks [ASSUMPTION].
- In the prototype Dana has no app of her own: her answer is **simulated** [BRIEF].

## Goals
- Decide in a few seconds, with the right context in front of her.
- Know exactly what she approves: which recipient, which sentence, which version.
- Leave a record of the decision.

## Frustrations
- Vague requests ("can you take a look?").
- Approving something that is then changed.
- Having to open the whole message to understand the question.

## Behaviour
- Reads on the phone between meetings [ASSUMPTION].
- Approves quickly what is clear; puts off what is unclear [ASSUMPTION].

## Typical risks and errors
- Approving without reading the reason for the request.
- Approving a version that the coordinator changes right after.

## What Dana needs from the interface
- A request that stands on its own: issue, recipient or sentence, reason, note, version.
- Two clear answers: **Approve** / **Decline**.
- In the prototype: "Answer as Dana Whitfield", which opens the request in the foreground under a clear simulation label (TC8 in [decisions.md](../../decisions.md)).
